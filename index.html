Welcome to ACT Lost and Found

The ACT Lost and Found System helps students, faculty and staff recover items lost on campus. Anyone who finds something can log it here, and anyone who loses something can search for it or report it. Lost reports and found items appear side by side so matches are easy to spot.

Claim guide

1. Bring your ID

A valid ACT school ID is required for every claim.

2. Prove it's yours

Describe a detail that isn't shown in the listing.

3. Claim within 60 days

Unclaimed items are donated or disposed of after that, except valuable items such as phones, laptops and jewelry, which are kept longer at the office.

How to use it

  1. 1Search first. Use the search box and filters to check the found items.
  2. 2No luck? Report it. Tap + New report and choose lost or found.
  3. 3Check matches. Open an item to read its details and request to claim it. Follow your reports under My reports.
  4. 4Claim in person. Bring your school ID to the office desk to pick it up.
